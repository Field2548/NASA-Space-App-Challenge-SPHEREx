The idea
2 / 7

Movers hide among millions of stars

SPHEREx maps the whole sky in 102 near-infrared colours.

Asteroids, dwarf planets and maybe a Planet X drift between visits.

Our trick: give each moment in time its own colour.

Asteroid (433) Eros over 5 moments. Still stars stay white; the mover leaves coloured dots. Circles: where JPL says Eros was.

Impact and next steps
7 / 7

A new NASA dataset, open to everyone

IMPACT

Anyone can explore SPHEREx in a browser and learn to spot movers.

Real data, honest noise bars, open code.

NEXT

Rate classifier: speed to distance, flag Planet X-slow movers.

Longer baselines: days to months for ~1 px/day motion.

Cross-match known objects with the Minor Planet Center.
